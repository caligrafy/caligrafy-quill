# 9.3 Metadata & Social Rich Cards

> Source: Caligrafy Quill wiki - https://github.com/caligrafy/caligrafy-quill/wiki/9.3-Metadata-&-Social-Rich-Cards
> Bundled with the caligrafy-quill Claude plugin. Content preserved from the wiki; formatting cleaned.

When someone shares an address of your app on Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Slack or iMessage, those apps show a **rich
card**: a title, a line of description and an image, read from the page's `og:` and `twitter:` meta tags. Every page
built on `default/master/site` prints the card of your app (2.16.2+); you set it once with `metadata()`, and a page can
have its own.

> #### (video) [Creating Rich Cards with Caligrafy](https://youtu.be/qgzufCEcg_I)
>
> In this video, we show how we can create Rich Cards that give search engines, social media networks and collaboration tools the ability to have more information about your site, application or product from the URL alone.

## With Claude

```text
Give Bookshelf its social card: "Bookshelf: your books, lent and returned", and each book page its own title
```

Claude fills the `metadata(...)` call of `application/config/config.php` and, for a page of its own, sets `$metadata`
in that view.

**Check**

+ The page source of `/books` has `og:title`, `og:description` and `og:image`.
+ The image is 1200 by 630 and its address is a full `https://` address.
+ The card shows in [opengraph.xyz](https://www.opengraph.xyz/) once the app is online.

## By hand

**1. The card of the app.** In `application/config/config.php`, replace the commented `metadata(...)` example with:

```php
metadata(array(
    'site_name' => 'Bookshelf',
    'title' => 'Bookshelf: your books, lent and returned',
    'description' => 'Know who has your books and when they come back.',
    'image' => 'https://bookshelf.example.com/public/images/resources/social-card.png',
    'image_alt' => 'Bookshelf',
));
```

Put a 1200 by 630 image at `public/images/resources/social-card.png`.

**2. Look at it.** Open the source of any page of `default/master/site` (`/books`, `/login`...). The `head` has, among the
image size, its `alt` and the other `twitter:` lines:

```text
<meta name="description" content="Know who has your books and when they come back."/>
<meta property="og:type" content="website"/>
<meta property="og:site_name" content="Bookshelf"/>
<meta property="og:title" content="Bookshelf: your books, lent and returned"/>
<meta property="og:description" content="Know who has your books and when they come back."/>
<meta property="og:image" content="https://bookshelf.example.com/public/images/resources/social-card.png"/>
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image"/>
```

With `APP_URL` set (Caligrafy Cloud sets it), `og:url` is that address plus the page's path.

**3. A card per book.** Each book page shows its own title and summary. It uses the Bookshelf page of one book
(`application/views/Bookshelf/book.pug`, [lesson 04](https://github.com/caligrafy/caligrafy-quill-course/wiki/04-Your-First-Capabilities)
of the course), whose route in `application/config/routing/web.php` gives it `$book` (`get_book`: the book with its
`author` nested) and `$authors` (`list_authors`):

```php
    Route::page('/books/{id}', 'Bookshelf/book', ['data' => ['book' => 'get_book', 'authors' => 'list_authors'], 'notFound' => 'Bookshelf/notfound']);
```

In `application/views/Bookshelf/book.pug`, make the first block set `$metadata` (the layout's `head` reads it):

```pug
block title
    - $metadata = array('title' => $book['title'] . ' on Bookshelf', 'description' => $book['summary']) + (array) metadata()
    = $book['title'] . ' · Bookshelf'
```

Leave the rest of the view (its `block content`) as it is.

Open the source of `http://localhost:8080/books/3`: `<meta property="og:title" content="Kindred on Bookshelf"/>` and
`<meta name="description" content="A young woman is pulled back in time to a plantation in Maryland."/>`; the image and
the rest come from `metadata()`. `$metadata` set outside a block (below `extends`) is not seen by the layout.

**4. Share links.** To show *Share: Facebook X LinkedIn Pinterest* under a page, add `'site_url' =>
'https://bookshelf.example.com'` to the `metadata(...)` of step 1 (the address the links share), and replace the
page's footer:

```pug
block footer
  footer.cq-footer
    include ../default/modules/share
```

The links share `site_url`, the app's address, not the page's.

**5. Test the card online.** Platforms read the card from the internet: on your computer, use a tunnel such as
[ngrok](https://ngrok.com/), or try it once the app is online. Then paste the address in
[opengraph.xyz](https://www.opengraph.xyz/), the [Facebook debugger](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/sharing/)
or the [LinkedIn post inspector](https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/). Platforms keep a card for days: after a
change, press **Scrape again** in Facebook's debugger. X has no validator any more: preview a post with the address.

**Check**

+ `og:title` and `og:image` are in the source of every page.
+ `/books/3` has its own title.
+ A debugger shows the image, the title and the domain.

## Reference: the card

`metadata(array $pairs)` keeps the pairs for the request (in the session) and returns them all; `metadata()` returns
them. A `null` value is ignored. `default/modules/metadata.pug` prints the card from the view's `$metadata`, else from
`metadata()`, and prints nothing without a `title`.

| Key | Becomes | |
|---|---|---|
| `title` | `og:title`, `twitter:title` | Required |
| `description` | `description`, `og:description`, `twitter:description` | Under 125 characters; X and LinkedIn do not show it |
| `image` | `og:image`, `twitter:image` (a large card) | A full address, 1200x630 |
| `image_width`, `image_height` | `og:image:width`, `og:image:height` | `1200` and `630` by default |
| `image_alt` | `og:image:alt`, `twitter:image:alt` | What the image shows |
| `site_name` | `og:site_name` | |
| `site_url` | `og:url`: this address plus the page's path; the address the share links share | `APP_URL` by default |

X and LinkedIn show only the image, the title and the domain: say what the app does in the title.

**`$metadata` in a view also prints a product block.** When a view of `site.pug` has `$metadata`, the layout adds
`default/modules/metadata_body.pug` to the page: hidden schema.org `Product` data (name, description, image, a price from
`amount`, `0.00` by default, a rating from `ratingValue` and `reviewCount`). It is meant for a page that sells one
product; give those keys their real values on such a page.

| Module | |
|---|---|
| `default/modules/metadata.pug` | the card, in the `head` of `site.pug` |
| `default/modules/metadata_body.pug` | the schema.org `Product` data, when the view has `$metadata` |
| `default/modules/share.pug` | share links for the footer of `site.pug` pages |
| `default/modules/social.pug` | share links of the older `layout.pug` |

## Other ways

**A controller you keep** passes the card to its view: `view('Bookshelf/book', array('book' => $book, 'authors' =>
$authors, 'metadata' => array('title' => $book['title']) + metadata()))`. `Route::page` and `$metadata` in the view replace it.

**The older masters** (`default/master/layoutblocks.pug`, `layout.pug`) print the card only when the view gets
`'metadata'`, and the share links when it also gets `'social' => true`:
`view('default/index', array('metadata' => metadata(), 'social' => true))`.

## Common mistakes

+ **No card at all.** `metadata()` has no `title`: nothing is printed without one.
+ **The image does not show.** The address is relative (`/public/...`) or the server is not reachable from the
  internet: use a full `https://` address of a server online.
+ **An old card keeps showing.** The platform kept it: *Scrape again* in Facebook's debugger.
+ **The per-page title is ignored.** `$metadata` was set outside a block: set it in the first block of the view.
+ **Share links share nothing.** `site_url` is not in `metadata()`.
